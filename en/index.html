Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your computer and, if you use the app, on your phone. When you turn on sync, also in your own Google Drive, encrypted with a key only your devices have. Nobody else sees it, not even the people who make Antdy.

Do I have to give my bank passwords?

No. Antdy works with the statements you download from your bank's website or app, or the ones that arrive by email.

Does it work with my bank?

It reads PDFs from BBVA, CaixaBank and imagin, and the Excel or CSV exports of BBVA, CaixaBank, imagin, Santander, Sabadell, ING, Openbank, Bankinter, N26 and Revolut. For any other bank, a Norma 43 file or a CSV with date, description and amount will do, and PDFs usually work too. It is designed for Spanish banks and euros.

Do I have to pay for Plus?

Not for now: Plus comes free in the app. When the shop opens, a single payment will give you Plus forever on up to three computers, with every update, and you'll be able to try it for 30 days before deciding.

What if I don't buy Plus?

You keep using everything free with no time or transaction limit, and your data stays where it is.

Windows shows a warning when I open the installer. Is that normal?

Yes. Antdy doesn't have a publisher signature yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. Always download it from this page.

Do I need to download it again to get new features?

No. Antdy updates itself and shows you what's new the next time you open it. Your data stays as it was.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Antdy runs on Windows 10 and 11, and the Android and iPhone apps are coming very soon.
